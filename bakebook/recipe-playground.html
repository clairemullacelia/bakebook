Country Sourdough Loaf

Active 40 min · Total 18 hr · Makes 1 loaf
72% hydration · overnight cold proof · a crackly, open-crumb boule built on a mature levain and a long, cold retard.
Ingredients
Amount
Ingredient
500 g
bread flour
◔
360 g
water, 30°C
◔
100 g
active levain
●

11 g

◔
50 g

Method
1
Mix flour and water, hold back the salt, and rest 45 minutes to autolyse.
◔
2
Add the levain and salt, then work the dough until it comes together into a smooth, cohesive mass.
◔
3
Bulk ferment about 4 hours at 24°C, giving the dough a set of stretch-and-folds every 30 minutes for the first two hours. You want it to grow roughly 50% in volume, feel airy and alive, and hold a soft dome when you nudge the side of the bowl — this is the step people rush, and rushing it is why a loaf comes out dense. Watch the dough, not the clock.
◔
4
Pre-shape, bench rest 20 minutes, then shape tight and place seam-up in a floured banneton.
◔
5
Cold retard overnight, 12–16 hours at 4°C.
◔
6
Bake 20 min covered at 250°C, then 20–25 min uncovered until deep brown.
